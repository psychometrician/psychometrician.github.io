RSS

Three feeds. Each carries the whole text of a post, not a summary.

Feed Address
Everything https://psychometrician.github.io/index.xml
R posts only https://psychometrician.github.io/index-rstats.xml
Julia posts only https://psychometrician.github.io/index-julialang.xml

Subscribe to the first one if you want everything. The other two are for R-bloggers and JuliaBloggers, which republish one language each, so a feed of everything would send them posts their readers did not ask for.

There is no Python or JavaScript feed, and the reason is about how those communities work rather than about the languages. R and Julia have sites that subscribe to a feed and republish from it. Python and JavaScript do not: their newsletters take one submission at a time, by form or by mail, so a feed there would have nobody at the other end.

Only the first address is announced in the page header, which is what a reader’s feed reader finds on its own. The other two are here because nothing else on the site links them.
